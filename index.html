🛠 Field Standard Operating Procedures (IRPWM & RDSO Standards)

1. Sleeper 03 & 04 Dowel Retrofit Procedure (Notes 25 & 26)

Where dowel holes are absent in outer ends of PSC Sleepers 03 & 04, drill 35 mm dia holes to 165 mm depth using rotary percussion drills. Flush with compressed dry air. Inject approved IS: 12994-1990 Type L-100 epoxy resin and insert RDSO/T-3002 dowels. Mandatory 24-hour curing is strictly required before driving plate screws or applying torque.

2. Detail 'B' Tie Bar Dropped Clearance Verification (Alt 12 & 13)

Verify that the M.S. Flat Tie Bar RDSO/T-9010 maintains its 222 mm drop bend with at least 12 mm clearance above the sleeper surface. The S&T Clamp Point Lock (RDSO/S-3454) drive rod must pass freely through the span without contacting the tie bar.

3. Spring Setting Device (SSD) Maintenance at JOH (RDSO/T-6216)

Inspect helical spring tension and turnbuckle adjustment at Sleeper 13. Ensure that minimum flangeway clearance of 60 mm is maintained at the Junction of Rail Heads (JOH) when the switch is thrown reverse.

4. Check Rail Flangeway Clearance Inspection (RDSO/T-6275)

Verify that check rail flangeway gap remains strictly between 41 mm and 45 mm (nominal 44 mm) opposite the CMS nose. Flare ends must maintain 89 mm opening to prevent wheel flange impact.
