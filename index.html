🛠 Senior Section Engineer (P.Way) Field Protocols

1. Sleeper 03 & 04 Tie Bar Dowel Retrofit (Note 25 & 26)

Condition: When retrofitting older PSC sleepers without pre-formed tie bar dowel holes.

  • Drill holes: 35 mm diameter, 165 mm depth at both ends of Sleeper 03 and 04.
  • Clean hole thoroughly of concrete dust using pneumatic air blower.
  • Fill hole with IS: 12994-1990 Type L-100 Epoxy Resin and insert Polyethylene Dowel RDSO/T-3002.
  • Mandatory Hold Period: Allow exactly 24 hours of undisturbed curing before mounting M.S. Flat Tie Bar RDSO/T-9010 and tightening Plate Screws RDSO/T-3913.
2. Welded Tongue Rail Joint Floating Sleeper Rule (Note 22 & Alt 11)

When rewelding the Stock Rail Joint (SRJ) in the field, Sleeper No. 1 must be shifted inwards slightly so the weld remains floating and not directly supported on the sleeper edge. Hole in the tie bar must be shifted to match.

3. Curvature Verification & Transit Distortion Rectification (Notes 20 & 27)

Check tongue and stock rail versines against IRPWM Annexure 4/6 & 4/7 prior to laying. Rectify transit distortions with approved hydraulic rail benders.
